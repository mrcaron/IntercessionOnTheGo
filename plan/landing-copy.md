# Landing page wording

**Status:** proposed on this branch (`landing-copy`), not merged. Written 2026-11-12. The greeting and "praying for you" were chosen by the owner on the same day.

## Goal

Make the rider's landing page more to the point and less wordy, and keep the emotional connection. The original voice is kept: "apprentice of Jesus", "God sees you and loves you deeply", and "the sign is that you saw me and got here". Every promise stays accurate: private, locked on the phone, never shared, sold or published, erases itself, can be bookmarked.

**Result:** 156 words down to 111 across the four main blocks (29% fewer).

## What changed

| Block | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Opening (48 → 37 words) | I am an apprentice of Jesus praying over you right now on the bus. I want you to know that God sees you and loves you deeply, in the midst of the joys and sorrows you are carrying. The sign is that you saw me and got here. | I'm an apprentice of Jesus, praying for you right now on the bus. God sees you and loves you deeply, in the joys and sorrows you carry. The sign is that you saw me and got here. |
| Invitation (48 → 32) | I would love to pray more specifically for your needs as you feel open to sharing them. Feel free to ignore me, or introduce yourself personally if you're comfortable. If you'd like to remain discreet, tell me below how to pray for you, or merely share your name. | I'd love to pray for what's on your heart. Share a need or just your name. Only I can read it. Or say hi in person, or ignore me; that's fine too. |
| Chat (8 → 5) | Anonymous, and not attached to any phone number. | Anonymous. No phone number needed. |
| Privacy note (52 → 37) | This is confidential and will not be shared with anyone else, sold or otherwise published anywhere. Only you and I can see this space: what you write is locked on your phone and only my phone can open it. You can bookmark this page and come back any time before it erases. | Private: only you and I can see this. What you write is locked on your phone and only mine can open it. It's never shared, sold or published. Bookmark this page to come back before it erases. |

Also tightened:

- After sending: "Sent. Keep this page open or come back to it, and you'll see here the moment I've prayed for you." → "Sent. Keep this page open, or come back to it: you'll see here when I've prayed for you."
- When off duty: "…so this page isn't taking requests at the moment." → "…so this page isn't open."

Greeting: "Hi there, I'm Mike." → "Hi, I'm Mike."

Unchanged: the field labels, the "Send it off" button, "Pause: keep it one more day", "Erase now", and the "This page erases itself in 1 h, with zero record." line.

## Reasoning

- **Cut the throat-clearing:** "I want you to know that" and "in the midst of" added length, not warmth. "God sees you and loves you deeply" now lands as a direct statement.
- **One invitation, three ways in:** the old paragraph listed four options (ignore me, introduce yourself, remain discreet, share your name). The new one gives the two that matter below (a need or just a name) and keeps both in-person options ("say hi in person, or ignore me").
- **Reassurance at the moment of decision:** "Only I can read it." now sits right where the rider decides whether to write. That is what "remain discreet" was doing, in four words, and it lets the privacy note at the bottom be shorter.
- **No new promises:** nothing says you will pray within a certain time, and no claim has been added that the old text did not make.

## Alternatives for the invitation

Pick one, mix, or ask for another round.

- **Warmer:** "I'd love to pray for what's on your heart, big or small. Share as much or as little as you like. Even just your name is enough. Only I can read it." (and drop the in-person line)
- **Shortest:** "Want me to pray for something? Tell me below, or just share your name. Only I can read it." (28 words → 20)
- **Keeps the in-person welcome more prominent:** "Say hi in person any time. Or tell me below what to pray for, or just share your name. Only I can read it."

## Decided

- **Greeting:** "Hi, I'm Mike." (shorter than "Hi there, I'm Mike.").
- **"Praying for you"** instead of "praying over you", which reads warmer to people who don't use the church phrase. The Settings preview line for the owner uses the same words.
